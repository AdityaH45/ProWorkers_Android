Delete your ProWorkers account

For customers and workers using the ProWorkers app or website.

Delete it yourself in the app

  1. Open ProWorkers and log in.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete my account.
  4. Enter your password and confirm.
Before you can delete: you must have no job in progress, no unpaid job, and (for workers) nothing left to pay ProWorkers or to withdraw. The app tells you if something is still open.

What is removed

  • Your name, phone number, email address, profile photo, saved location and notification token.
  • Your login. You will no longer be able to sign in.

What is kept

  • Booking and payment records, without your personal details, where the law requires it or where a payment or complaint is still open.

Can't open the app?

Email proworkers.support@gmail.com from the email address on your account with the subject "Delete my ProWorkers account". We will confirm and delete it, usually within a few days.
