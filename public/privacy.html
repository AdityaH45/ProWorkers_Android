Privacy Policy

ProWorkers · Surat, Gujarat, India
Last updated: 26 October 2026

ProWorkers connects customers with electricians, plumbers and labour workers. This page explains what information we collect when you use the ProWorkers app or website, why we collect it, who can see it, and what choices you have. We keep it in plain words on purpose.

In short: we collect only what we need to match customers with workers, show where the worker is, take payments and keep the service safe. We do not sell your personal information. We do not show ads.

1. Who we are

"ProWorkers", "we", "us" means the ProWorkers service run from Surat, India. To contact us about your information, write to proworkers.support@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

WhatWhy
Name, email, phone number, passwordTo create your account and let you sign in. Your password is stored only in scrambled (hashed) form; we cannot read it.
Profile photo (optional)So the other person on a job can recognise you.
Address and map pin (customers)So the worker can find you. A booking's address is shown to workers who are offered the job. Your phone number is shared with a worker only after that worker accepts.

Chat messagesMessages between a customer and a worker about a booking. They are deleted automatically when the booking is finished and paid, or cancelled.

Worker payout detailsA worker's UPI ID and withdrawal history, so we can pay earnings out.
Device and notification dataA notification token (to send job and payment alerts) and basic technical data such as app errors.

3. How we use it

  • To create accounts, match customers with workers, and complete bookings.
  • To show worker location and arrival to the customer during a job.
  • To take payments, confirm them, and pay workers.
  • To send alerts about bookings, payments and payouts.
  • To handle complaints, prevent fraud and misuse, and keep customers and workers safe.
  • To keep records the law requires, and to improve the service.

4. Who can see your information

  • The other person on your job. Customers and workers see what is needed to do the job together: name, photo, rating, phone number (after acceptance), and the address or live position as described above.
  • ProWorkers admins can see accounts, bookings, payments and complaints to run the service and settle disputes.
  • Service providers that run the app for us: cloud hosting (Render), database (MongoDB Atlas), website hosting (Cloudflare), push notifications (Google Firebase), email delivery (Google), and map and place search (Mappls / MapmyIndia). They handle data only to provide their service to us.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it or to protect people's safety.

We do not sell your personal information to anyone.

5. How long we keep it

  • Chat messages and problem photos: removed automatically when the booking ends (finished and paid, or cancelled).
  • Worker live location: only the latest position is kept, and it is cleared when the worker goes offline.
  • Account details, booking and payment records: kept while your account is active and for as long as needed for accounts, tax, disputes and legal reasons.

6. Your choices and rights

  • You can edit your name, phone, email and photo in the app's Profile.
  • You can turn off location in your phone's settings, or (for workers) go offline. Workers need location on to receive nearby jobs.
  • You can turn off notifications in your phone's settings.
  • You can ask us to see, correct or delete your information, or delete your account, by emailing proworkers.support@gmail.com from the email address on your account. We will reply within a reasonable time. Some records may be kept where the law requires or where a payment or complaint is still open.

7. Security

Passwords are stored hashed, connections to our servers use HTTPS, and access to admin tools is protected with a second step. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password and do not share it. Tell us straight away if you think your account was misused.

8. Children

ProWorkers is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you think a child has given us information, write to us and we will delete it.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the service changes. The date at the top shows the latest version. If a change is important, we will tell you in the app.

10. Contact

Questions or requests about your information: proworkers.support@gmail.com
